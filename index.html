Who We Are

A Legacy of Firsts in Indian Weaving Accessories

Established in 2005 by Mr. K V. Suthar and Mr. Bhavesh Suthar, Mikado Enterprise Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a vision to manufacture high-quality, cost-effective and technically precise weaving accessories. Chairman Mr. K V. Suthar carries over 30 years of experience and is regarded as an icon in the weaving accessories field.

  • First in India to manufacture profile Reeds
  • First to import automatic SPALECK machinery from Germany for SS Heddles
