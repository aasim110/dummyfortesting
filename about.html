Three Decades of Weaving Expertise, One Company Since 2005

Mikado Enterprise Pvt. Ltd. was founded to manufacture high-quality, cost-effective and technically precise weaving accessories for the global textile industry.

Our Company

Built on Vision, Driven by Precision

Our Company, Mikado Enterprise Pvt. Ltd., was established in 2005 with a view to manufacture high quality, cost effective and technically precise weaving accessories.

The Chairman, Mr. K V. Suthar, is an icon in this field and carries an experience of over 30 years in the subject of weaving accessories. He had the vision to bring many firsts to India:

  • First in India to manufacture profile Reeds
  • First to import fully-automatic “SPALECK” machinery from Germany for the manufacture of SS Heddles, matching the best international quality
  • Many other special application reeds, combs and heddles

His son, Mr. Bhavesh Suthar, has been involved in R&D, adaptation of the latest technology and automation in manufacturing over the past decade. Their invaluable experience, combined with up-to-date technology, superior quality raw material and stringent quality tests, make our products unique.

Mikado Enterprise Pvt. Ltd. has state-of-the-art technology with imported machinery from Europe. We are located at Modasa — about 100 km from Ahmedabad — and employ highly trained staff with excellent work practices.
